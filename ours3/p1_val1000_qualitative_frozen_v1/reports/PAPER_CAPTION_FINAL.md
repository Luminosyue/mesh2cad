**Figure X. Qualitative P1 results on representative cases from the EXACT9998 VAL1000 validation set.** Predictions are mapped back to the GT canonical CAD frame using frozen coordinate transforms. All methods share the same GT bounding box and camera, with identical tessellation settings; no per-prediction auto-fitting, recentering, or rescaling is used. The examples illustrate structural and geometric recovery, including both successful and failure/limitation cases. Curved surfaces shown here come from circle-based extrusion rather than explicit fillet/chamfer operations; therefore, this figure should not be read as evidence of Fillet or Chamfer recovery.
