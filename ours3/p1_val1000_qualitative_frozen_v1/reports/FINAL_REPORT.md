## Rendering contract

Prediction geometry was mapped back to the GT canonical CAD frame using the frozen coordinate transform. Shared GT bounding box and shared GT camera were used for GT, A1, A3, and P1. There is no per-prediction auto-fit, recenter, or rescale. The render protocol uses common tessellation and an orthographic camera.

## Interpretation guardrails

- Prediction and evaluator outputs were not modified.
- Curved surfaces visible in the figure must not be interpreted as evidence of Fillet or Chamfer recovery.
- VAL1000 GT operation inventory has Fillet / Chamfer / Revolve / Arc count = 0.
- The main cylindrical/curved surfaces are primarily produced by circle-based extrusion (`Extrude(Circle(...))`).
- Some structures that look approximately circular are generated by polygonal approximation (`ExtrudePolygon`), not by a true circle operation.

Accordingly, the figure supports qualitative discussion of structure and geometry recovery on representative cases, with explicit limits on operation-level claims.
