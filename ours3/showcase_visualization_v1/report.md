# SHOWCASE_VISUALIZATION_V1

Five showcase cases were rendered from frozen artifacts only.

- Mesh panel: source OBJ shaded with triangle wireframe.
- Split panel: P3/CAD parts for multi-part cases; source normal families for whole-object cases.
- B-rep panel: SHA-verified STEP face tessellation and true TopoDS edge curves extracted after fresh STEP reload.
- The B-rep panels do not contain source mesh geometry.

Caveats: CASE_05 remains 95.6067% rather than exact 100% and is loft-heavy. REAL_03 is the strict topology-complete showcase.
